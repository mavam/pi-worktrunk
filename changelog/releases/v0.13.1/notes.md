This release fixes the expand shortcut hint on collapsed Worktrunk results. Pi 1.5 users now see their configured key instead of a hint without one.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

### Configured expand shortcuts in Worktrunk results

Collapsed Worktrunk results and `/wt` command cards now show your configured expand shortcut in Pi 1.5, including custom keybindings. Previously, the hint could appear without a key.
